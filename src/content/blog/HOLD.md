I am stressed just thinking about this. Where will I be in November 2027, roughly one year from now?

Will I let life direct me as it has done throughout most of my life? Will I let the river carry me away, floating downstream, entranced by the sunshine?

Or will I turn my back on the sun, open my eyes to the turbulent waters, and swim upstream? 

My mind, which is usually pretty good at fantasising about a life in which all my goals are fully realised [REF], seems scared of even imagining where I might be just one year from now. Am I only allowed to wildly fantasise about a timeless future?

A predictably boring explanation for this anxiety is that next year is close enough for me to feel its weight. Next year will come, and I will be held accountable. The inquisitor will hammer relentlessly on the inside of my skull [REF]. And I don't want to deal with that. I am tired of carrying the weight of my own expectations on my shoulders. But I am also tired of living a predictable life with unrealised dreams. Living a jester life.

Let's start with the obvious outcome: the predictable path.

I will be working at the same company. It is unlikely that I will get promoted. I've decided to reduce my take-home pay to increase my contributions to the "I've given up" pot, a sign of cowardice masquerading as a "just in case" strategy. I guess people who rose above conventional work didn't spend much time thinking about "just in case", right?

I will probably continue to go to the gym, but not as regularly as I would like to force myself to. I will probably move within the same narrow band of progress, reaching the same mediocre peak as always before slipping back to the bottom whenever I lose motivation or replace the gym with running or something else. Body unchanged, except for the aches I seem to collect with age.

I will spend most of my time consuming the same short-form of entertainment, fuelled by the endless stream of novelty from anonymous users. Retention of 5%, without any lasting learning. Feeling guilty, I will read a book about how to optimise what's fundamentally wrong.

Working from 8am to 6pm. Dinner finished by 8pm. Weekends free to run errands, eat pastries and drink coffee. Holidays abroad visiting tourist spots, museums, art galleries, and trying new dishes. It feels bland and boring just writing about this.

That's it. A good, comfortable life of monotony and quiet. Day in and day out. 

Would you allow me the insolence of predicting a slightly different path? It is uncharted and foggy, I know, but it is as far into the future as I currently dare to see.

- I will get promoted.
- I will secure the secondment abroad.
- I will start learning Mandarin and stick with it.
- I will run a marathon.
- I will resolve the aches I've collected.
- I will have a body I feel more comfortable in.
- I will give more in my relationship.
- I will keep writing.

God, please let me fail or win. I don't care. I just want an outcome.

Let's see how I measure up in a year's time. 

See you then, future me.
