I've wanted somewhere to collect my thoughts for a while.

I spend a lot of time learning things, investigating questions, changing my mind,
trying different approaches, and occasionally going quite deep into subjects that
interest me.

Most of that disappears.

This website is an attempt to change that.

## What I want to write about

I don't want this website to have a narrowly defined subject.

I expect to write about things I'm genuinely trying to understand or do.

That might include learning Mandarin, engineering, career development, technology,
personal experiments, books, or something completely different.

## What I don't want this to become

I don't want to manufacture advice just because I have a website.

I'd rather document what I'm actually doing, what I thought beforehand, what
happened, and whether my thinking changed.

## An experiment

For now, that's all this website is: an experiment.

I'll see what it becomes.
