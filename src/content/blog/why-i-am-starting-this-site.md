I've wanted somewhere to collect my thoughts for a while.

I spend a lot of time learning things, investigating questions,
changing my mind, and trying different approaches.

Most of that disappears.

This website is an attempt to change that.

## Why write publicly?

Writing forces me to articulate ideas that otherwise remain vague.

I also want a record of how my thinking changes over time.

## What I want to write about

I don't want this website to have a narrowly defined subject.

### Learning

I expect to document things I am actively trying to learn.

Mandarin is an obvious example.

### Engineering

I also want to write about engineering concepts that I find interesting,
provided I can discuss them without using confidential information.

## What I don't want this to become

I don't want to manufacture advice simply because I have a website.

I'd rather document what I'm actually doing, what I thought beforehand,
what happened, and whether my thinking changed.

## An experiment

For now, that's all this website is: an experiment.

I'll see what it becomes.

One of the sites that influenced how I want to approach long-form
writing is Gwern Branwen's website.[^gwern] and Isaak blog [^isaak]

[^gwern]: Gwern Branwen, [*Spaced Repetition*](https://gwern.net/spaced-repetition).
