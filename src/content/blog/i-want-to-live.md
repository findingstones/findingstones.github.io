I want to live.

I want to live through the troughs and peaks of our civilisation and see what lies at the end of the road.

Will we destroy ourselves? Will we become incompatible with our environment? Will we transcend our own biology and transfer our existence to another reality?

I want to know.

Humanity has an insatiable appetite. We are an endless well of unmet needs, constantly reaching for more. More knowledge, more power, more control. Yesterday's impossibilities become today's mundane conveniences, and tomorrow's ambitions become delirious.

I believe in human supremacy. I believe humanity should place its own advancement above everything else. The world around us is something to understand, exploit, transform and ultimately bend to our purposes. Even if, in the process, we cease to be human.

Humanity is the true religion whose god has yet to be born.

We are its worshippers, devoted to more. We destroy whatever stands in our way and build upon its remains. Each generation inherits the machinery and books of its predecessors, only to dismantle them and build something greater.

We are its sacrifice. Everything is an offering at the altar of advancement. Everything is expendable in the pursuit of what we might become. I believe extraordinary achievements justify any cost.

And where might it lead?

I want to believe we will overcome our biological machinery. We will rewrite our genetic code and develop medicines so potent they can keep our bodies in perfect health. Our cognitive abilities will be maximised. We might even discard our bodies altogether, replacing them with something beyond the fruits of millions of years of evolution.

And we will not stop there.

We will explore the universe, confront its mysteries and seek to master whatever forces govern it. We will push towards controlling the code behind our reality. And we will reach the source code behind that code.

And we will not stop there.

We will eat reality itself and, in the empty spaces left behind, place our wildest dreams.

Yet I don't have much faith we will get that far.

We might destroy ourselves, reach an insurmountable obstacle beyond our comprehension, discover that our ambitions lie beyond the limits of the universe, or end up trapped in a permanent stagnation.

Perhaps humanity will be no more than a curiosity. A god that never came to be.

Nevertheless, I root for humanity. I want to see our mysteries unravel and our certainties crumble.

I pray that human ingenuity can spare us from our biological deterioration. That we might be granted enough time to witness even a fraction of what lies ahead.

Let me tell you something: don't die.
